# Controls

All keyboard and gamepad bindings can be remapped in **Settings → Controls**.
Each action can have two keyboard bindings and one gamepad binding. Binding a
key that is already in use removes it from the other action (you are told
which).

| Action | Keyboard / mouse (default) | Gamepad (standard layout) |
|---|---|---|
| Move | W A S D | Left stick |
| Camera | Mouse (click the game to capture the cursor) or arrow keys | Right stick |
| Sprint (hold) | Left Shift | L3 (press left stick) |
| Dodge roll | Space | B / Circle |
| Light attack (press repeatedly to combo) | Left mouse button or J | X / Square, or RB |
| Heavy attack | Right mouse button or K | Y / Triangle, or RT |
| Spirit ability | Q | LT |
| Interact / talk / advance dialogue | E | A / Cross |
| Use Healing Dango | F | D-pad Up |
| Lock on / release | Middle mouse or Tab | R3 (press right stick) |
| Switch locked target | Mouse wheel down or T | D-pad Right |
| Swap weapon | R | LB |
| Inventory | I | D-pad Down |
| Quest log | L | Back / Select |
| Pause menu | Esc or P | Start |

## Menus

* Mouse: click.
* Keyboard: arrow keys or Tab to move, Enter to confirm, Esc or Backspace to go back.
  Left and right arrows change a highlighted setting.
* Gamepad: D-pad or left stick to move, A to confirm, B or Start to go back,
  LB/RB to switch tabs.
* Dialogue choices: arrows or W/S to move and Enter or E to choose; or press the number key (1–9).

## Combat basics

* **Red ground markings** show exactly where an enemy attack will land. They
  fill up as the attack winds up. In the final moments the enemy flashes red.
  Step out of the marking or **dodge through it**: you are invulnerable for most of
  the roll.
* Light attacks chain into combos. Heavy attacks do more poise damage and
  **stagger** enemies, leaving them open.
* Landing hits fills the blue **spirit** bar. Spend it on your weapon's
  spirit ability (each weapon family has its own).
* Carry two weapons and swap mid-fight. Swapping can cancel an attack's
  recovery.
* Expanding shockwave rings can't be outrun. Roll through them.
* Settings → Accessibility has high-contrast warnings (yellow/magenta) and
  captions for important sounds. Settings → Gameplay has a Story difficulty.
